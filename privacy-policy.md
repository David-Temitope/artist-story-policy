# Privacy Policy for Genesis

**Last Updated: November 2026**

This Privacy Policy describes how **Genesis** ("we", "us", or "our"), an Android simulation game (Package ID: `com.artiststory.game`), handles information when you use our mobile application.

We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring transparency regarding our data practices.

---

## 1. Information We Collect

### A. Personal Information
**Genesis does NOT collect, store, or transmit any personally identifiable information (PII).** 
* We do not collect your real name, email address, phone number, physical address, or IP address.
* The game does not require account creation, registration, or third-party sign-in.

### B. Device & Local Data
* **Local Game Saves:** All gameplay data, including your simulated artist profile, stats, songs, virtual inventory, and in-game decisions, are stored strictly locally on your device storage. We do not transmit or sync your game progress to any remote server or cloud database.
* **Camera & Media Access:** If you choose to customize your artist's avatar or album cover art using your camera or photo gallery, this image data is processed and stored locally on your device only. We do not upload, collect, or share your photos or camera captures.

---

## 2. In-Game Simulation vs. Real Financial Features
Genesis includes simulated elements such as virtual banking, cryptocurrency trading, record label contracts, and in-game advertising/sponsorships. 
* All monetary values, stocks, transactions, and advertisements within the game are **100% fictional simulations**.
* The game does not connect to real financial institutions, does not process real money, and does not conduct real-money transactions.

---

## 3. Advertising & Analytics
* Genesis does **not** contain third-party ad networks (such as Google AdMob, Unity Ads, etc.).
* Genesis does **not** use third-party behavioral tracking or analytics SDKs to track your activity across other apps or websites.

---

## 4. Third-Party Services & Links
The app operates offline and does not integrate third-party trackers. If any external links are accessed via standard web intents, please review the respective privacy policies of those third-party websites or services.

---

## 5. Children's Privacy
Genesis does not knowingly collect or solicit personal information from children under the age of 13. Because our application does not collect personal information of any kind, we comply with the requirements of COPPA (Children's Online Privacy Protection Act) and the EU GDPR.

---

## 6. Data Security & Storage
Because all your game data is stored locally on your device:
* Your data remains on your device until you uninstall the application or clear the app's cache and data via your device settings.
* We recommend using your device's built-in backup tools (such as Google Drive device backup) if you wish to retain your saved game data across device migrations.

---

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last Updated" date. You are advised to review this page periodically for any changes.
